# IRB Saudi Arabia — go-to-market and discovery plan

Revision: 5 September 2026. This is a launch preparation plan and a set of reviewable campaign assets. It does not establish that a public launch, advertising campaign, regulatory authorization, or production security assessment has occurred.

## Positioning

**English:** Research ethics workflows, from paper to a clear digital record. Prepare applications and documents with AI assistance; qualified human reviewers retain decision authority.

**العربية:** إجراءات أخلاقيات البحث، من الورق إلى سجل رقمي واضح. أعدّ الطلبات والمستندات بمساعدة الذكاء الاصطناعي، مع بقاء صلاحية القرار لدى المراجعين البشريين المؤهلين.

The product is independent workflow software focused on Saudi Arabia. Do not describe it as the national regulator, an official NCBE/NBCE portal, a licensed IRB, or an accredited ethics committee without current documentary evidence covering this operator, institution, activity, and jurisdiction. Institution-specific ethical authority must be established separately from software access.

International expansion from **2027 is a roadmap**. Each market requires a local applicability assessment, qualified reviewer arrangements, institutional acceptance, data-processing and transfer arrangements, and operating readiness. Existing certificates do not automatically become globally valid in 2027.

## Launch sequence

1. **Operator readiness:** publish the actual controller identity, support and privacy contacts, responsible institutional committee, appointment/qualification evidence, processing purposes and lawful bases, provider list/locations, transfer safeguards, retention schedule, pricing/terms, and complaints process. Close the repository release and operational gates with evidence.
2. **Controlled Saudi pilot:** invite a small set of qualified institutional users. Exercise an entirely de-identified application through preparation, submission, reviewer assignment, decision, document verification, amendment, and revocation. Have the bioethics lead review content and operating procedures in Arabic and English.
3. **Public information launch:** configure the purchased domain, HTTPS, canonical origin, email identity, search verification, and public support. Check the deployed robots, sitemap, public page HTML, private-route noindex, response headers, and link validity. Publish transparent service scope and actual pricing only after the offer is approved.
4. **Paid acquisition pilot:** use approved institutional search intent and operator-approved budget caps. Route traffic to the public homepage/resources, then a clear application or support action. The operator controls campaign creation, audience, spend, and publication. No ad account or campaign is created by this document.
5. **Scale after operational evidence:** expand only when reviewers meet the agreed service commitment, support can handle demand, abuse costs remain within budget, incident response is rehearsed, and successful outcomes are supported by evidence. Review campaign and operating results weekly during the pilot.

## On-site SEO

- `shared/seo.ts` is the route contract: public page titles/descriptions in English and Arabic, indexable public paths, strict HTTPS canonical-origin validation, and shared factual questions/answers.
- `client/index.html` provides a safe fallback; build-generated public route HTML must apply the route-specific title, description, canonical and robots values. Unknown/private routes remain noindex.
- Use only the purchased and configured `VITE_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` HTTPS origin for canonical and sitemap URLs. Do not derive canonical hosts from an untrusted request Host header. Do not publish a placeholder domain.
- Canonicals follow the actual public route; private application URLs, chat, account, admin, registry and individual certificate references are excluded from sitemap/indexing.
- Public navigation/footer links are real anchors where used for discovery. The homepage and resources share visible FAQ copy, and the guidelines link to primary authorities. Metadata must agree with visible content.
- The language switch changes the interface and page language. It does **not** establish independently crawlable `/ar` and `/en` URLs; do not emit fabricated hreflang pairs. Dedicated language URLs and equivalent server-rendered pages are a future, separately verified SEO improvement.
- Configure Search Console/Bing Webmaster ownership using the real domain after purchase. Submit the generated sitemap only after production crawl verification. Search rank and index inclusion are not guaranteed.

## Measurement without research-data exposure

Third-party analytics scripts are intentionally not injected in the research SPA: a script loaded on a marketing page can retain access after navigation into a private application. This also removes session replay/debug collection from the shipped public assets.

The optional first-party page counter requires `VITE_PUBLIC_ANALYTICS_ENABLED=1`. It honors Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control, permits only named public information routes, strips query/hash data, excludes record verification and private routes, stores its random session ID only in page memory, and sends no heartbeat. This is a minimization control, not a legal-basis determination. Enable it only after the actual privacy notice and processing basis are established.

Measure operational conversion using authorized, aggregate server reports: qualified registrations, completed drafts, submitted applications, first human review, decisions, correction rate, support time, generation success, latency, rate-limit events, and AI cost. Keep investigator names, email addresses, protocol titles, document bodies, and IRB reference numbers out of campaign event payloads. Do not use certificate downloads as ad conversions or researcher protocols as audience attributes.

Performance/operation targets must be documented separately from measurements: e.g. public p75 LCP ≤2.5 seconds, INP ≤200 ms and CLS ≤0.1 are targets, not results from this audit. A synthetic local browser pass is not a production load or field-performance test.
