Conditions of Approval

This approval is granted on the condition that the Principal Investigator and all associated personnel comply with the obligations enumerated below. Any deviation requires prior written notification to the IRB Secretariat.

Investigator Obligations

  1. Adherence to protocol. The study shall be conducted strictly in accordance with the approved protocol; substantive amendments require prior IRB review.
  2. Adverse events. Serious adverse events shall be reported to the IRB within 7 calendar days of investigator awareness.
  3. Informed consent. The approved consent form shall be used in the language of the participant; consent shall be documented in writing.
  4. Data integrity. Source documents shall be retained for a minimum of fifteen (15) years from study closure, in accordance with NBCE Article 26.
  5. Annual continuation review. A continuation report is due not later than thirty (30) days prior to the expiry date noted on Page 1.

Participant Safeguards

  1. Voluntariness. Participation is wholly voluntary; withdrawal at any stage shall not prejudice the participant's standing or care.
  2. Privacy & PDPL. Identifiable data shall be processed in accordance with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law and stored on Kingdom-resident infrastructure.
  3. Risk minimisation. The Investigator shall implement the risk-mitigation measures described in §6 of the approved protocol.
  4. Vulnerable populations. Where applicable, additional safeguards under NBCE Article 14 shall apply throughout the study.
  5. Right to information. Participants retain the right to access study-relevant information about themselves at any time.
